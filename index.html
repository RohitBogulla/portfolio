// about

I build systems that move data reliably, then teach models to make sense of it.

I'm a Machine Learning Engineer and Computer Science graduate student at the University of Florida, with 4+ years of industry experience at Oracle building and operating large-scale, production systems. I specialize in end-to-end AI systems — from research and model development to deployment, optimization, and monitoring in real-world environments.

My work spans Graph Neural Networks, NLP, Vision-Language Models, and LLM systems, with a strong emphasis on explainability, scalability, and performance. That ML work is grounded in years of building low-latency, high-throughput distributed systems at Oracle — which strongly informs how I design reliable, production-ready ML pipelines today, including a comic-accessibility project I submitted to ACL 2025.

I like the seam between backend engineering and ML — the same instincts that keep a Kafka pipeline reliable at 10M events a day are what make an inference pipeline trustworthy in production.
